# Imagem do login

- Asset: `public/images/login-cinema.webp`
- Geração: ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, modo generate (sem imagens de referência).
- Dimensões entregues: 1672 × 941 px, aproximadamente 16:9.
- Arquivo final: WebP, qualidade 85, 345.906 bytes. A imagem PNG original foi preservada fora do repositório.
- Direção: mosaico cinematográfico de pôsteres fictícios para fundo da tela de login; o escurecimento e a interface são aplicados em CSS.

## Prompt utilizado

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Full-viewport cinematic image background for a premium streaming service login page.
Primary request: An elegant wall of approximately 18 original fictional film and television posters, with absolutely no text, arranged in a dense edge-to-edge grid of portrait rectangles. Wide landscape 16:9 composition, ideally 2304 by 1296 pixels.
Scene/backdrop: The poster wall itself fills the entire image with narrow dark gutters; rows run at a subtle diagonal angle with restrained perspective, like an expansive streaming catalogue artwork mosaic.
Subject: Distinct fictional cinematic poster artworks including an astronaut and a distant planet, an intense female thriller portrait, a rain-soaked neon city, a racing car at night, a luminous soccer stadium, majestic mountains, an underwater ocean documentary with a whale, a colorful animated adventure, desert explorers, and moody drama close-ups. Each poster is its own carefully art-directed composition.
Style/medium: Premium theatrical key art, predominantly photorealistic with sharp photographic detail and rich restrained contrast. A few animated film posters provide variety.
Composition/framing: Many varied portrait artworks visible across the entire frame; cropped posters continue beyond all four edges, tightly organized with subtle diagonal rows, no flat empty areas.
Lighting/mood: Immersive, dramatic cinematic lighting; retain enough midtone brightness and image detail because a dark CSS overlay will be applied separately.
Color palette: Deep teal and blue, warm amber, selective deep red highlights, natural skin tones.
Constraints: All fictional original characters and scenes. No recognizable celebrities, existing movie characters, franchise imagery, titles, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, icons, buttons, form fields, browser frame, or other UI. This is only the background artwork, not a webpage mockup.
```

## Revisão visual

Mosaico inspecionado após a geração: retratos, ficção científica, esporte, animação, aventura e documentário com detalhes claros e contraste cinematográfico. Sem títulos de filmes ou componentes de interface; eventuais letreiros da cidade são parte da arte. O asset ocupa toda a área e pode ser recortado com object-fit: cover.
